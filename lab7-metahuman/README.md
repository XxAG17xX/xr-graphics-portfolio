# Lab 7: A MetaHuman of Myself in Unreal Engine 5.6

> My face, scanned, turned into a fully rigged digital human and animated from my own voice

<img src="../media/lab7_metahuman.gif" width="70%" alt="The MetaHuman speaking">

**Demo:** [facial_animation.mp4](facial_animation.mp4) (24 s, narrated)

## What was asked
Scan your face, clean the scan, build a **MetaHuman** from it in **Unreal Engine**, customise it to look like you, and show it animated.

## What I did
- Solved my face scan into a **MetaHuman Identity**, conformed a MetaHuman Character to it and ran **Create Full Rig** in **MetaHuman Creator**.
- Generated the facial animation with **MetaHuman Animator** from audio of my own voice, then played it on the character in a level with Sequencer.

## Results
- The mouth, lips and cheeks follow the speech, and the teeth show on open vowels (see the video).

## Files
- `facial_animation.mp4`: the result. `screenshots/`: the Identity solve, the rig and the level.

## What I would change
Fix the skin poking through the T-shirt when the head moves, and the dark rings around the eyes under the level's lighting.
